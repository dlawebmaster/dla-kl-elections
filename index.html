Adding another advisor

Advisor accounts are managed in the Supabase project “DLA Kappa League Elections App.”

  1. In Supabase, open Authentication, then Users, and add a user with the advisor’s email and a password. Turn on auto-confirm.
  2. In the SQL editor, run: insert into kl_advisors (email) values ('advisor@example.com'); with their email.
  3. Share the page link. They sign in with “Advisors: sign in here” at the bottom of the members’ screen.

Kappa League election

Constitution of the Kappa Instructional Leadership League (Kappa League), sponsored by Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc.

Article I – Name

Section 1. The name of this organization shall be the Kappa Instructional Leadership League, to be called the Kappa League.

Article II – Purpose

Section 1. The objectives of this organization shall be to promote and develop the leadership potential of its members through participation in club-oriented activities and, at the same time, motivate them to raise their aspiration level.

Article III – Membership

Section 1. The organization’s membership shall consist of male students properly enrolled, who meet the specifications of the membership of the Kappa League.

Section 2. All members shall be privileged to participate in all Kappa League activities and elections, except when said privilege is suspended by reason of the members’ failure to maintain the minimum requirements.

Article IV – Officers

Section 1. The officers of the organization shall be a president, first vice-president, second vice-president, third vice-president, secretary, treasurer, and five directors of Area Phases.

Section 2. A presidential vacancy shall be filled in order of succession, beginning with the first vice-president, the second vice-president, and finally, if necessary, a special Kappa League election.

Section 3. These officers shall hold office for no more than one year.

Section 4. The entire membership will elect all officers during elections held in May of each year.

Section 5. The qualifications and duties for each office are to be determined by the Kappa League members’ Bylaws as approved by the Kappa League members.

Section 6. No officer may hold more than one seat in the Kappa League for the duration of one year.

Article V – Executive and Legislative Powers

Section 1. The legislative powers shall be vested in the Board of Directors.

Section 2. The Board of Directors, consisting of the officers listed in Article IV, shall meet regularly and, by majority vote, shall pass Kappa League programs and projects to be carried out by the Kappa League. By majority vote, the Board of Directors shall recommend proposed laws and regulations for that purpose.

Article VI – Amendments

Section 1. Either the Kappa League membership or the Board of Directors may draft proposed amendments to this Constitution.

Section 2. Upon the arrival of most members of the Kappa League Board of Directors, the proposed amendment must be approved by a 2/3 vote cast by the Kappa League membership in a special Kappa League meeting called expressly for that purpose.

Article VII – Bylaws

Section 1. The membership, by majority vote, shall determine the Bylaws of the Kappa League, which must not conflict with the content of the Constitution of the Kappa League.

Article VIII – Ratification

Section 1. The Constitution shall take effect when a 2/3 vote cast by the Kappa League membership ratifies it.

Article IX – Sponsorship

Section 1. The Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc., sponsors the Kappa League.

Section 2. The Kappa League shall engage in no programs or projects unless approved by the Fraternity appointed advisors.

Section 3. Without the fraternity-appointed advisors’ written approval, the Kappa League shall make no expenditure.

Bylaws of the Kappa League.

Article I – Duties of Officers

Section 1. The President shall:

  1. Act as the chief executive officer of the Kappa League.
  2. Preside as a nonvoting member at all Board of Directors and General Membership meetings.
  3. Be an ex-officio member of all committees of the Kappa League.
  4. Be empowered to appoint any special committees for the general welfare of the Kappa League.

Section 2. The First Vice-President shall:

  1. Be in charge of programs and projects.
  2. Be an ex-officio member of all committees of (a) immediately above.
  3. Be an assistant to the President for all executive tasks.

Section 3. The Second Vice-President shall:

  1. Be in charge of the following areas of responsibility: Parliamentarian, Sergeant-at-Arms, Constitution, Bylaws, Historian, and Public Relations.
  2. Be an ex-officio member of all committees of (a) immediately above.
  3. Be an assistant to the President for all executive tasks.

Section 4. The Third Vice-President shall:

  1. Be in charge of membership recruitment and activation.
  2. Be an ex-officio member of all committees of (a) immediately above.
  3. Be an assistant to the President for all executive tasks.

Section 5. The Secretary shall:

  1. Keep minutes of all Board of Directors and General Membership meetings.
  2. Maintain a book of records containing the minutes listed in (a) immediately above.
  3. Handle all correspondence for the Kappa League.
  4. Be assisted by a corresponding and recording secretary whose duties shall be assigned by the Secretary and will have the power to appoint members to this position.

Section 6. The Treasurer shall:

  1. Be chairman of the Ways and Means Committee.
  2. Collect and record all dues and assessments.
  3. Be an ex-officio member of all fund-raising committees.
  4. Be assisted by a collection and recording Treasurer whose duties and appointments shall be made by the Treasurer.

Section 7. The Historian shall:

  1. Record all activities of historical interest in the Kappa League scrapbook.
  2. Maintain the Kappa League archives.

Section 8. The Director of Public Relations shall:

  1. Direct the Community Contact Committee of the Kappa League.
  2. Arrange the publicity for all Kappa League events.
  3. Direct the artwork and photography as it relates to publicity.
  4. Be in charge of the Kappa League bulletin board and/or showcase.

Section 9. The Parliamentarian shall:

  1. Make all decisions in parliamentary procedure.
  2. Direct all Kappa League elections.

Article II – Qualifications for Office

Section 1. The Kappa League President must be a senior class member when in office and must have served at least one previous year on the Board of Directors.

Section 2. The First Vice-President must be in the senior class when serving in office.

Section 3. All other officers must be in at least the 10th grade when serving in office and have been a Kappa League member for at least six months.

Section 4. To be eligible for any Kappa League office, members must have at least a “C” average with no “Failures,” acceptable deportment, cooperation, and work habits as recorded on the last official report card.

Article III – Meetings

Section 1. The Board of Directors will meet before each General Membership meeting. All committee chairmen proposing to make a report at the General Membership meeting must submit their report to the Board. Any Kappa League member is entitled to attend the Board meeting.

Section 2. The General Membership meetings will be held twice a month. One-third of the membership constitutes a quorum to hold a meeting.

Section 3. All meetings will be held at .

Article IV – Participation Requirements

Section 1. Minimum requirements for the privilege to participate in Kappa League activities and programs:

  1. Officers are required to attend each called meeting except when excused by the Kappa Advisor in advance of the time of said called meeting.
  2. New members must attend three (3) General Membership meetings in succession to be eligible for a Membership card.
  3. Any member absent from General Membership meetings for two successive General Membership meetings shall lose his voting rights.
  4. Any member absent from General Membership meetings for three successive General Membership meetings shall lose his privilege to attend all Kappa League activities. Such members shall be reinstated only after having applied to the Board of Directors and said application approved by the Board.
  5. Officers shall be excused from not more than two (2) Board of Directors meetings and one (1) General Membership meeting during his term of office.

Section 2. Dues

  1. Dues shall be per General Membership meeting. Members becoming delinquent in dues shall become subject to Article IV, Section 1, d, above.

How it works

Follow these steps to run a Kappa League election from start to finish.

1

Sign in as the advisor

  • The page opens on the members’ screen. Tap “Advisors: sign in here” at the bottom.
  • Sign in with your advisor email and password. Accounts are added in Supabase (see the Advisor tab).
  • When you’re signed in, you see the advisor tabs. Signing out returns the page to the ballot.
2

Set up the election

  • Go to Nomination, type the election name, and tap “Save details.”
  • Tap “Choose offices,” check the offices on this year’s ballot, and tap “Add selected offices.”
  • Review the qualifications for office.
3

Open nominations

  • On Voting, tap “Open nominations,” then share the page link with members.
  • Members running for office open the link, pick the office, and add their name and grade.
  • On Nomination, check each submission against the qualifications and tap “Approve” or “Decline.” Approved names go on the ballot.
  • You can also add nominees yourself under each office.
4

Open voting

  • On Voting, tap “Close nominations and open voting.”
  • Anyone with the link can vote, once per phone or computer. They choose one nominee per office, or “No vote,” then cast their ballot.
  • Voting in person on one device? Tap “Open shared ballot screen” so members can vote one after another.
  • Want a stricter election? Turn on “Require voting codes,” add the roster, and hand out the printed code slips.
5

Close voting and check results

  • When everyone has voted, tap “Advisors: sign in here” and sign back in.
  • Go to Voting and tap “Close voting.”
  • Go to Results to see the winner of each office. Ties are flagged for a runoff.
6

Create the report for the records

  • On Results, tap “Create report.”
  • Add the election date, who prepared it, and any notes.
  • Download it as Word, PDF, or for Google Docs.
  • Print it, have the Parliamentarian and Chapter Advisor sign it, and file it.

Good to know

  • Ballots are anonymous. The app records who voted, but not how they voted.
  • Tap “Sign out and return to ballot” any time you step away from the device.
  • To start over, use “Clear all votes” on the Voting tab. Offices, nominees, and the roster stay.
  • Lost voting code? Sign in, find the member on the roster, and tap ↻ to give them a new one.
  • Forgot your password? Whoever manages the chapter’s Supabase project can reset it.
